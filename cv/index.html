Professional Summary

I am an M.S. student in Robotics Engineering at WPI, working with Dr. Mahdi Agheli in the ALMaS Research Group. I study how legged robots choose where to touch and how hard to push. I lead SCRUB, in which our armless Unitree Go1 presses a tool against a wall or column that the robot has no model of and slides the tool along it. With Owen Sullivan, I also work on GaitNet, a learned planner that picks each footstep for a quadruped crossing ground full of holes. My research interests are legged robotics, whole-body control and robot learning, and I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Projects

  • Humanoid ice skating with reinforcement learning (Jul 2026 – present)

    I train a simulated Unitree G1 to skate with PPO on thin passive blades, using a contact model I set up so each blade glides along its length and grips sideways. Trained in mjlab with no motion capture, reference motion or imitation reward, policies from three seeds had mean speed errors of 0.11 and 0.16 m/s at 0.6 and 0.9 m/s commands, with 3 to 4 cm/s of sideways blade slip. Everything so far is in simulation. The linked video shows my newer Isaac Lab version, which is trained to follow a reference gait recorded from an earlier policy of mine that I had shaped toward a human-like stroke.

  • Humanoid bimanual lifting with residual AMP-PPO (May 2026 – Jul 2026)

    This is a collaboration with Mohamed Shuaib Nasurudeen, who built the training stack; the code is in his repository. A Unitree G1 learns in MuJoCo simulation to grasp a box with both arms and lift it. My part was the residual version of the lift policy, a second network whose output is added to the actions of the frozen locomotion policy. I set it up, made the curricula adapt to the lift success rate and wrote most of the posture rewards. In simulation the converged policies hold the box at the goal height, within 3 cm of the target position. A lifting policy from the project has also run on the real G1, where it lifted a box (a qualitative result).

  • Conditional flow matching policies for robot manipulation (Apr 2026 – May 2026)

    I wrote a Conditional Flow Matching policy for robomimic and compared it with Diffusion Policy on the same U-Net backbone, on five simulated tasks from low-dimensional state with three seeds each. Success was comparable. Flow matching was ahead on Square and slightly behind on Tool Hang and Transport, and both reached 100% on Lift and Can. With 10 Euler steps it samples an action chunk about 11× faster than Diffusion Policy’s 100-step DDPM sampler.

  • Acrobatic quadrotor flight with L1 adaptation (Oct 2025 – Dec 2025)

    For my Robot Control course, I wrote an SE(3) geometric controller with an L1 adaptive layer for a simulated Crazyflie in ROS 2 and Gazebo. It flies a 360° roll flip in place and recovers to hover. In one logged run, position RMSE was 2.2 cm in x and y and 7.0 cm in z, and the L1 layer cancelled 94%, 86% and 71% of an injected step torque on roll, pitch and yaw. I have not flown it on hardware.

  • Autonomous rover for the ISRO Robotics Challenge 2024 (Dec 2022 – Aug 2024)

    A six-wheeled rocker-bogie rover that mapped terrain with ORB-SLAM, planned kinodynamic paths and ran on custom control electronics.
