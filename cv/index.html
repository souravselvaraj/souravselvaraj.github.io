Projects

  • Simulated Ice-Skating Locomotion for Humanoids via Blade-Contact RL (Jul 2026 – present)

    Unitree G1 trained with PPO in MuJoCo to skate on passive 3 mm blades without imitation data; the skating gait emerges from anisotropic blade–ice contact. Tracks a 0.9 m/s command within 0.09 m/s at 94% single support.

  • Bimanual Lifting for Humanoids via Residual AMP–PPO (May 2026 – Jul 2026)

    Collaborated on training a Unitree G1 to lift objects with residual AMP–PPO over a frozen locomotion actor, holding them within 3 cm of target in simulation using adaptive curricula and dynamics randomization, then run on the real G1 with a box.

  • Conditional Flow Matching Policies for Robot Manipulation (Apr 2026 – May 2026)

    Conditional Flow Matching visuomotor policy built from scratch in robomimic — 11× faster inference than 100-step DDPM Diffusion Policy on the same backbone, with equal or higher success on the tasks compared head-to-head.

  • Acrobatic Quadrotor Control with L1 Adaptive Augmentation (Oct 2025 – Dec 2025)

    SE(3) geometric controller with L1 adaptive augmentation for a Crazyflie 2.1 in ROS 2 simulation — 2.2 cm position RMSE, up to 94% of force-step disturbances rejected.

  • Autonomous Rover — ISRO Robotics Challenge 2024 (Dec 2022 – Aug 2024)

    6-wheeled rocker-bogie autonomous rover with ORB-SLAM terrain mapping, kinodynamic path planning, and custom control electronics.

  • Additional projects

    Visuomotor imitation learning — Behavior Cloning vs. Diffusion Policy on robosuite (code); task-and-motion planning — PDDL + OMPL block assembly on a Franka Panda.
