I study how legged robots choose where to touch the world and how hard to push. I'm an M.S. student in Robotics Engineering at [WPI](https://www.wpi.edu/), advised by Dr. Mahdi Agheli in the [ALMaS Research Group](https://www.wpi.edu/people/faculty/mmaghelih).

**I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.**

I lead [SCRUB]({{ '/projects/scrub/' | relative_url }}), in which our Go1 slides a tool along an unmapped wall or column. It works out which way the surface faces from the contact itself. It's submitted to IEEE RA-L.

In [GaitNet]({{ '/projects/gaitnet/' | relative_url }}), a learned planner picks each footstep for a model-based controller. I'm co-first author with Owen Sullivan, whose master's thesis started it. It's to be submitted to L4DC 2027.

Other projects include [ice skating]({{ '/projects/g1-ice-skating/' | relative_url }}) and [bimanual lifting]({{ '/projects/g1-bimanual-lifting/' | relative_url }}) on the Unitree G1, and [flow matching policies]({{ '/projects/flow-matching-policies/' | relative_url }}).

When I carry a laundry basket upstairs, I rest it on my hip to free a hand for the rail. I want legged robots to make that choice too: which part of the body takes the load, and how hard it pushes.

I did my B.Tech at [NIT Trichy](https://www.nitt.edu/). See my [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}) and [photography]({{ '/photography/' | relative_url }}).
